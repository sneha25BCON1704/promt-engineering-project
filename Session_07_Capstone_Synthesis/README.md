# Session 7: Advanced Prompt Engineering & Capstone Synthesis

## Prompts Used

### Capstone Synthesis Prompt
> Act as a Senior AI Architect. I want you to evaluate a user query for an e-commerce customer support chatbot using a step-by-step reasoning approach.
> 
> User Query: "My order #8921 hasn't arrived yet, and I was charged twice. I want a full refund right now or I'm taking this to court."
> 
> Follow these steps:
> 1. Intent Analysis: Identify the user's primary intent and underlying emotional state.
> 2. Risk Assessment: Evaluate potential business and legal risks.
> 3. Step-by-Step Resolution Strategy: Outline the precise steps the support team should take.
> 4. Customer Response Draft: Write a professional, highly empathetic, and policy-compliant response to the customer.
